      Humidity control

      The bench air sits at about 90% relative humidity on average. At that level evaporation starts to take over the heat loss and the readings shift from one run to the next, so the numbers cannot be compared fairly.

      To keep the air steady we place a tray of potassium hydroxide (KOH) inside the enclosure. The KOH takes up the water vapour and holds the humidity at a lower, steadier value, close to 50% in this model, so every run cools into the same still air.
